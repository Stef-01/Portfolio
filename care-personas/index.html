A precision-persona project for health-system design

One health system.
Many personas.
Zero average patients.

Precision public health proved that a population is never one audience — it's five or six personas, each needing a different intervention. This project applies the same segmentation logic to the health system itself: to the specialist's appointment book, and to the patients who never found their family GP. Each explainer ends the same way — with indicative economics and a fundable trial.

EXPLAINER 01

The Specialist-GP Pipeline

Five caseload personas inside every specialist's waiting room — and a complexity screen that moves the bottom 20% to accredited GPs with an eConsult backstop. GPs bill more, specialists bill more, the waitlist halves.

The shared method

1. Segment — replace the “average patient” with named personas, sized as shares of a real book or population. · 2. Match — assign each persona the clinician tier or relationship it actually needs. · 3. Align the money — show, in indicative dollars, why every stakeholder earns more doing it. · 4. Ask small — end with a single fundable trial and hard KPIs.

CARE PERSONAS

A standalone concept product — two interactive explainers making the case for persona-driven care models, built for health-system decision-makers. All figures indicative; every page ends in a trial designed to replace assumptions with measurements.

Format and segmentation approach inspired by Surgo Ventures' COVID-19 vaccine persona explainer. All personas illustrative; all demo clinicians fictional.
